# Name and logo

The code is free software under the AGPL-3.0-or-later (see `LICENSE`). The **names "Mitra Home" and "Mitra Home"
(the project's earlier name, still used for the repository, folders and task names) and the logo are not covered by that
licence**; they identify the official project and its signed releases.

## You may, without asking
- Use, study, change and share the code under the AGPL, including running it for your own family or for friends.
- Say your work is "based on Mitra Home" or "a fork of Mitra Home" (or FamilyAgent), with a link to this repository.
- Write about the project, review it, and link to it.

## Please do not
- Name a fork, product or service "Mitra Home" or "Mitra Home" (or something that could be mistaken for it), or use the logo in a way
  that suggests your version is the official one or endorsed by the author.
- Publish releases signed to look like official ones. Official releases are signed with the project's key
  (`RELEASING.md`); a fork signs with its own key and says so.

If you want to use the name for something else, ask first (see `SECURITY.md` for how to reach the maintainer).
This note is a plain-language statement of intent, not legal advice.
