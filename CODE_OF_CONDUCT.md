# Code of conduct

Mitra Home is built for families, and this project should feel like a place families would be comfortable in.

## Expected
- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not developers; plain questions get plain answers.
- Assume good intent, criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Keep family data private: never post anyone's bank details, account numbers, documents, addresses or children's
  information, your own included. Use the invented test family (Alex, Sam, Biscuit) in examples.

## Not accepted
- Harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or pushing paid services in issues and discussions.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem
privately to **cloudwareedge@gmail.com** with "Conduct" in the subject; reports are kept confidential.
